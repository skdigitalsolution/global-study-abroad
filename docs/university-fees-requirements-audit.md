# Universities: fee and English-requirement audit

Reviewed: 2026-10-28 (28 October 2026).

Updated all 20 previously-placeholder records in the existing 28-university directory. Existing eight legacy records were preserved exactly, not recertified by this audit. University names, IDs, locations, images, programmes, study levels, order, numeric sort keys, search/filter logic and actions were preserved. No new universities or routes.

Only official university sources are used below. The scope is deliberately specific: selected degree examples do not establish a university-wide fee or language threshold. UG = undergraduate; PG = postgraduate; tuition excludes living costs and may exclude compulsory charges. Dates belong to each source, not a common assumed intake. The directory includes multiple currencies; existing tuition sorting has no currency conversion and was left unchanged.

## Values still requiring a programme-level fee check

- Sorbonne University: international fee status/exemption eligibility prevents a universal annual total.
- Université Paris-Saclay: official page still shows 2025/26 national rates; 2026/27 publication pending on that page.
- University of Bologna: fixed instalment verified, variable programme/income contribution prevents a common annual maximum.
- Politecnico di Milano: instalments verified, but taxes/credits/income/non-EU status affect the annual total.
- University College Dublin: official registry page was accessible; its embedded fee-system frame was not retrievable. No amount invented.
- Universiti Malaya: published CS amount is the whole-programme total, not annual. No annual division inferred.

## English-language limitations

No common numeric IELTS cutoff was verified for Heidelberg, Sorbonne, NUS, Tokyo or current KAIST UG intake; these are explicitly course/faculty/qualification/intake-specific. Kyoto iUP explicitly publishes no fixed English-test minimum; that is not an absence of language requirements. Paris-Saclay AI and Bologna Business & Economics use verified programme-specific CEFR B2 evidence rather than an invented IELTS conversion. NTU's numeric example is explicitly limited to its Australian-qualification route. Each other value is labelled by scope.
